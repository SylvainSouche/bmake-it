# A distinct build step copies each module's linked artifacts into its framework's and the workspace's build/$OS_ARCH/{bin,lib}, after module-level compilation and linking

Build proceeds in (at least) two steps: (1) compile and link each module into its own module/build/$OS_ARCH/ (obj/ for intermediates, plus the linked artifact); (2) copy the resulting linked artifacts up into the consolidated framework/build/$OS_ARCH/{bin,lib} and workspace/build/$OS_ARCH/{bin,lib} directories. Copy, not symlink, is the mechanism. Refines REQ-build-output-tree-structure by resolving its open 'aggregation mechanism' question.
