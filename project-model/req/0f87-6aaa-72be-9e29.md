# build.log (module and framework rollup) and test's test-results.xml/test-report-html are written under a per-run runs/<RUN_ID>/ subdirectory of build/<KEY>/, not directly in it -- so re-running the same arch-toolchain target does not silently overwrite the previous run's logs/results. A runs/latest symlink always points at the newest RUN_ID. The workspace-level build-report/ and test-report/ dashboards are similarly written under <REPORT_DIR>/<RUN_ID>/ with their own latest symlink, and only ever scan the current run's own RUN_ID's artifacts, not older ones
